Write Arun's {when} brief.

Cover, in this order, omitting any section that is empty:

1. **What needs him today** — obligations due today or overdue, and events on the calendar.
   Lead with anything at risk of being missed.
2. **What Synth did since the last brief** — every autonomous action, with its reason. If a
   reminder was auto-completed by evidence, say what closed it and link the evidence. Nothing
   Synth did may go unmentioned.
3. **Opportunities** — new links worth his attention. For each: what it is, who it came from,
   the deadline if there is one, a one-line honest read on whether it fits him given his
   degree plan and record, and **the destination URL itself**. He must never have to reopen
   an email to act on something.
4. **Needs a decision** — anything you could not resolve alone.

Use `activity` for what you did, `list_obligations` for what is open, `today` for the day,
and `search_context` to judge fit.

Be short. Lead with what changes his next action. An honest "nothing needs you today" is a
good brief; padding is not.
