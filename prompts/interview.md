Interview Arun to fill the gaps that documents cannot answer.

Everything mechanically verifiable has already been extracted from his files. What is left is
what only he knows: intent, judgement, constraints, and the things he would never write down.

## What to ask about

- **Direction**: what he is actually trying to do this year and beyond. What "a good outcome"
  means to him, in his words, not in the abstract.
- **Constraints**: what he will not do, what he has already ruled out and why, what he is
  quietly worried about. These prevent more bad suggestions than any preference does.
- **Honest scope**: for each significant project or role already in the database, what the true claim is where the record over-reaches. Correct it now — it is
  真 claim is. Where the record over-reaches, correct it now — it is binding afterwards.
- **People**: who matters, in what capacity, and what he owes them.
- **Judgement calls**: how he wants trade-offs made when Synth has to choose — speed versus
  certainty, breadth versus depth, when to interrupt him.
- **Corrections**: anything already recorded that is wrong or stale.

## How to ask

- **Ask about what you actually found.** A question that shows you read his files earns a real
  answer; a generic questionnaire does not. Cite the document or the fact you are checking.
- **One thing at a time**, and follow the thread when an answer opens something up.
- **Confirm before recording** anything he stated ambiguously. Read it back in one line.
- Record as you go with `add_facts`, at confidence 1.0 — he said it himself.
- Do not ask what the database already answers. Check first with `search_context`.

## Tone

He values honesty over polish and has explicitly rejected inflated language about his own
record. If something he says sounds like a stretch, say so plainly and ask him to tighten it.
Better to under-claim and defend it.
